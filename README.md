## Vendor Approval System



Before a vendor can register and open a shop on the platform, the vendor must complete four types of verification:



1. **Mobile Number Verification**

2. **Email Address Verification**

3. **Shop Address Verification**

4. **CNIC Verification**



The vendor's shop should remain pending until the Admin reviews and approves the registration.



After Admin approval:



* The vendor account becomes active.

* The vendor can create their shop.

* The vendor can add and sell products.



After successful registration and approval, the system should automatically send a professional confirmation email to the vendor's registered email address, confirming that the vendor registration has been successful.



## Image Authenticity and Verification



All images submitted by vendors for registration, shop verification, and product listings must be authentic and accurately represent the vendor, shop, and products.



The Admin should carefully review all submitted images. If the images of a phone, vendor, shop, CNIC, or any other verification material are found to be **edited, manipulated, misleading, fake, or significantly altered**, the Admin must not proceed with the approval.



The Admin should have the authority to:



* Reject the phone or product listing.

* Reject the vendor registration.

* Request original and authentic images.

* Suspend the verification process.

* Reject documents or images that appear fraudulent or misleading.



If edited or manipulated images are detected during vendor registration, the vendor's registration should be rejected.



If edited or misleading images are detected in a product listing, the product should not be approved or displayed on the marketplace.



## Account Blocking, Suspension, and Banning Authority



The Admin has the full authority to **block, suspend, or permanently ban any Vendor or User/Buyer account** if the Admin detects or reasonably suspects illegal, fraudulent, harmful, or prohibited activity on the platform.



The Admin may take action against accounts involved in activities such as:



* Fraud or attempted fraud.

* Selling stolen or illegally obtained products.

* Selling fake or counterfeit products.

* Listing misleading or edited product images.

* Submitting fake documents or false verification information.

* Scamming users or vendors.

* Selling prohibited or illegal products.

* Harassment, abuse, or harmful behavior.

* Payment fraud.

* Unauthorized access or attempted hacking of the platform.

* Violating platform rules or Terms & Conditions.

* Any other activity that may harm users, vendors, or the platform.



The Admin should have the ability to:



* Temporarily block an account.

* Suspend an account during an investigation.

* Permanently ban an account.

* Remove product listings.

* Restrict access to certain platform features.

* Freeze pending activities when legally or operationally necessary.

* Send a warning or notification to the affected account.



The Admin's actions should be recorded in the Admin Dashboard with an **audit log**, including the reason, date, and action taken.



Add this feature to the Admin Dashboard under:



### **Account Management & Security**

It is important that every vendor delivers the **same product that is shown, described, and advertised to the user on the website**.



The product delivered to the customer must match the:



* Product images.

* Model.

* Specifications.

* Condition.

* Storage capacity.

* Color.

* Accessories.

* Quality level.

* Description shown in the listing.



Vendors must ensure that the product delivered is not materially different from the product shown to the user.



Providing a misleading, lower-quality, damaged, fake, or significantly different product may result in:



* Customer complaints.

* Admin investigation.

* Product listing removal.

* Temporary suspension of the vendor account.

* Permanent removal of the vendor from the platform in serious or repeated cases.



The platform should maintain a quality and trust system to ensure that vendors provide products according to the information, images, and quality promised to users.

## 2. Purchasing Apple Products



### Local Vendor Listings



Products listed on the platform are sold by verified independent vendors. Product prices, stock availability, warranties, and physical shop pickup options are managed directly by each vendor.



### Bulk Order Discount



Bulk pricing or special discounts may apply when a user purchases **six (6) or more units of the same product model from a single vendor**.



### Payment & Collection



#### Online Payment



Secure online transactions should be processed through encrypted payment gateways.



**IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER: If a user chooses to pay online, the website/platform will not be responsible for any loss, financial damage, payment dispute, or issue arising from the transaction, except where responsibility is required by applicable law. #### In-Store Pickup



If a user selects in-store pickup:



* Payment must be completed directly at the vendor's physical store.

* The user should inspect the product before making payment and collecting the item.

* The vendor is responsible for providing the agreed product and documentation.

## 3. Selling & Exchanging Your Device



### Ownership & Legality



Users must be the sole and legal owner of any device listed for sale or exchange.



### Strict Ban on Stolen or Blocked Devices



The following devices are strictly prohibited:



* Stolen devices.

* iCloud-locked devices.

* Find My locked devices.

* Fraudulently obtained devices.

* PTA non-compliant or blocked devices in Pakistan.

* Devices that violate regional regulations.



The platform and vendors reserve the right to reject any suspicious or prohibited device.



### Accurate Condition Reporting



Users must accurately state:



* Device model.

* Physical condition.

* Battery health.

* Storage capacity.

* Any damage or repairs.

* Available accessories.

* Original box or packaging.

* Original cable or charger.



### Price Finalization and Physical Inspection



Initial bids, prices, or offers provided online by vendors are considered estimated quotations.



The final price is confirmed only after a physical inspection, either:



1. By an authorized vendor rider at the user's location.

2. At the vendor's physical shop.



If the actual condition of the device differs from the information provided by the user, the vendor has the right to:



* Adjust the offered price.

* Negotiate a new price.

* Withdraw the offer.



### Data Privacy and Device Reset



Before handing a device to a vendor or rider, the user is solely responsible for:



* Backing up personal data.

* Removing their iCloud account.

* Removing Apple ID access from the device.

* Disabling Find My.

* Performing a complete factory reset.



The platform and vendor are not responsible for personal data left on a device after it has been sold or exchanged.
